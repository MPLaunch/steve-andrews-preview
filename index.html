The photographer

Chasing the light around Australia.

Steve is a landscape and wildlife photographer based on the New South Wales Central Coast. His work is about the quiet moments most of us walk past — first light on the coast, mist rising through the gums, a lone tree holding the last of the sun.

Every image in this collection was made in the field, then carefully printed and finished so it can live on your wall exactly the way it looked through the lens.
